Featured
Healthcare
Air quality testing & HVAC commissioning

Netcare Hospital Group – HVAC Commissioning

Full air quality testing and HVAC commissioning across multiple Netcare hospital facilities in Gauteng.

MS Engineering was appointed to conduct comprehensive air quality testing and commissioning of HVAC systems across several Netcare hospital facilities. The scope included AHU inspections, duct pressure testing, airflow balancing, and compliance verification against SANS standards. All work was completed on schedule with zero patient disruption.

  • Multiple hospital sites across Gauteng
  • Full SABS & ISO compliance verified
  • Zero operational disruptions during works

2010 FIFA World Cup commissioning

SAFA / FIFA House – 2010 World Cup

Air conditioning commissioning for South Africa's landmark FIFA World Cup facility.

A landmark achievement – MS Engineering was trusted to commission and test air conditioning systems for the SAFA/FIFA House ahead of the 2010 FIFA World Cup. This high-profile project required precision execution, strict adherence to international standards, and seamless delivery under significant time pressure.

  • International-standard HVAC commissioning
  • Delivered on tight FIFA event schedule
  • Full MEC systems verified and signed off

  • Operator training and as-built documentation
